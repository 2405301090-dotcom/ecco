🌿 100% Plastic-Free Craftsmanship

From Nature, For Nature

EcoPot was founded in 2022 with a simple mission: replace plastic plant pots with beautiful, biodegradable alternatives. Every pot we create is made from sustainably sourced materials — coconut coir, rice husk, bamboo fiber, and organic clay.

Unlike plastic pots that persist in landfills for centuries, our pots naturally decompose, enriching the soil they return to. And because they're breathable, your plants actually grow healthier in them.

Our Process

From Farm to Your Home

Every EcoPot goes through a mindful, low-impact production process.

01

Source

We collect agricultural byproducts — coconut husks, rice hulls, and bamboo offcuts — from local farms. Nothing goes to waste.

02

Process

Raw materials are cleaned, shredded, and mixed with natural binding agents like cassava starch and natural latex.

03

Mold & Shape

Artisans hand-press the mixture into molds, creating each pot with care and attention to detail.

04

Dry & Cure

Pots are sun-dried naturally — no energy-intensive kilns. This gives each pot its unique texture and character.

05

Ship Sustainably

We use recycled cardboard packaging with zero plastic. Shipping is carbon-offset through our reforestation partners.
