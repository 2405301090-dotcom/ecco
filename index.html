About EcoPot

A small pot with a big purpose.

How It Works

From seed to soil in
four simple steps

Fill

Add potting soil to your EcoPot, leaving a little space at the top for watering.

Sow

Plant your seed or seedling into the soil and water it gently until moist.

Grow

Keep it in good light and water regularly as your plant thrives and roots expand.

Transplant

Place the whole pot into the garden or a bigger container. It biodegrades naturally.
